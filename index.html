About the data
PermitClear is a limited beta testing tool. Results are generated from public government pages, official municipal-code sources, and public agency guidance. Bainbridge Island / Kitsap currently has the deepest local policy coverage. Sources are monitored for availability and changes. The tool is not an official government service, legal opinion, or permit decision, and is not being promoted for general public use at this stage.
